## Why

One well-tested path is more useful than several rough ones. The foundations themselves don't depend on the stack. Only the component steps assume it.

## When you'd choose otherwise

You use another framework, another styling approach, or a native platform.

## If you override it

Tokens, DESIGN.md, and the decision log work the same. Component steps are supported more loosely, and the agent adapts them to your stack.
