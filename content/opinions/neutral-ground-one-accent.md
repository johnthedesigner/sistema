## Why

Color is the strongest signal on a screen. When the accent is spent only on what can be acted on and what is selected, people find those things at a glance, and status colors stay readable as status. A slight tint in the neutrals keeps the interface from looking default without competing. This is where the color question starts, not where it must end: the brand and tinted options take color much further.

## When you'd choose otherwise

The brand is part of the experience, as in consumer, entertainment, or marketing products, and the person chose the tinted option or more in the direction.

## If you override it

Answer the color question with a stronger option. The direction records it, and the accent review rule follows that answer.
