## Why

Some choices, like a default purple gradient, are signs that nobody decided. Flagging them helps. But a flagged choice can be the right one for your product, and only you can tell.

## When you'd choose otherwise

You want a stricter review, such as for a brand with firm rules.

## If you override it

Record in DECISIONS.md which signals your project treats as blocking.
