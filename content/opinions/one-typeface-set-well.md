## Why

Most of a product's character comes from one typeface set well, not from many. Medium weight separates labels without the heaviness of bold; tighter tracking keeps large text from looking loose; tabular figures keep columns of numbers aligned.

## When you'd choose otherwise

The product needs a second voice, such as a display face for marketing pages or editorial headings, or a monospace face for code.

## If you override it

Add the second face in the type voice question and say what it's for. Record it in DESIGN.md.
