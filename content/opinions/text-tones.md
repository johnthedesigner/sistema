## Why

Secondary and tertiary tones carry a lot of hierarchy without adding size, weight, or lines: a record's title in the primary tone, its metadata in secondary, timestamps in tertiary. Apple's guidelines name the same ladder (label, secondary, tertiary). Pure black on white is harsher than it needs to be and leaves no room above for emphasis.

## When you'd choose otherwise

A brand with a defined text color, or a high-contrast product where every text tone must be as strong as possible.

## If you override it

Record the text tones in DECISIONS.md, with the reason.
