## Why

Direction and foundations are decisions about your product. An agent can propose them and check them. It shouldn't settle them.

## When you'd choose otherwise

You're working alone and are happy to approve in the same message you review in.

## If you override it

Approvals can be quick, but each one is still logged with a date. Skipping them entirely means nobody decided, which makes later changes harder to reason about.
