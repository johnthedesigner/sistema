## Why

A number that's right for one product is wrong for another. Standards already set these numbers for different uses. Sistema's rules take their values from the standard your project chose.

## When you'd choose otherwise

You want a number stricter than your standard requires.

## If you override it

Record it in DECISIONS.md as a project rule. The checks then use your number.
