## Why

Alignment and rhythm are felt more than seen. Linear's redesign treated the alignment of labels, icons, and buttons as something people feel after a few minutes. Dense content still needs room around it, or the whole screen feels cramped.

## When you'd choose otherwise

The project has an existing grid, such as 8px, that its code already follows.

## If you override it

Record the base unit in DECISIONS.md; the spacing play uses it.
