## Why

Typical values are useful starting points. They come from common cases, and your case may not be common. Stating the context lets you judge whether a number fits.

## When you'd choose otherwise

You want a guide's number enforced as a check.

## If you override it

Record it as a project rule in DECISIONS.md, and the agent applies it from then on.
