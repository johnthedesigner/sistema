## Why

A shadow says "this is above the page" and draws attention, because closer things are noticed first. On every panel it stops meaning anything and adds noise; Geist names over-elevating as a common source of visual noise. Kept for what really floats and for the one thing that should lead, it carries meaning. Layered shadows from one light source look natural instead of like blurry borders.

## When you'd choose otherwise

The person chose shadow as the way panels separate in the direction, or the product leans on physical metaphors, as many consumer mobile apps do.

## If you override it

Answer the separation question with "shadow". Panels then take the quiet lift, and the featured shadow stays stronger.
