## Why

A status color is a signal, and a signal works best small. A colored icon or edge on a neutral panel says "error" or "done" as clearly as a filled block, without turning every notice into an alarm or pulling the eye away from the work. Small marks like chips can take a tinted fill because they are small. In a dark theme, large status fills become deep, saturated blocks, so this keeps messages calm in both themes.

## When you'd choose otherwise

A blocking alert that must interrupt, such as data loss or a security warning, can take a filled block, used rarely enough to keep its force.

## If you override it

Record which messages use a filled block, and why, in DECISIONS.md, and check them in both themes.
