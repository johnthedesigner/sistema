## Why

Mixed icon sets have different grids, corners, and weights, which reads as unfinished. A stroke weight that matches the text keeps icons and labels of a piece.

## When you'd choose otherwise

A brand with its own icons, or a product that needs a specialist set (maps, medical) beside a general one.

## If you override it

Record each set and what it's used for in DECISIONS.md.
