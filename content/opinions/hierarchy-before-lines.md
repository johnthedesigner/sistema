## Why

Every line on a screen competes with the content for attention. When panels, chips, menus, and rows all carry outlines, the screen reads as a grid of boxes and the things that matter don't stand out. Space and a small change of tone separate groups as clearly, and quietly. A border is still the right tool where the edge itself tells you something: the field you type in, the checkbox you click, the row you follow across a wide table. Current systems agree: Fluent separates sections with space before lines, and Apple's guidelines name space, containers, and separators as alternatives to choose between.

## When you'd choose otherwise

The product's character is built on visible structure, such as a deliberately ruled, editorial or technical look, or the people using it work in conditions where tone differences don't show (low-quality displays, bright light).

## If you override it

Record in DECISIONS.md which surfaces carry outlines and why. The border-function review rule then accepts those surfaces.
