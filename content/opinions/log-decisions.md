## Why

Without reasons, a later person or agent can't tell a deliberate choice from an accident. They either undo it or are afraid to touch it. Agents read DECISIONS.md, so a logged decision keeps shaping their work.

## When you'd choose otherwise

Your team already keeps decisions somewhere else, such as architecture decision records.

## If you override it

Point AGENTS.md to where decisions live, so agents still read them. An override of an opinion is a decision like any other, and it names the opinion it overrides.
