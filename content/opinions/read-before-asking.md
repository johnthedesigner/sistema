## Why

Your repo already answers many questions: the framework, existing colors, fonts, and file layout. Asking you for them wastes your time. Asking also invites answers that don't match what's actually there.

## What breaks without it

The agent works from your memory of the project instead of the project. Existing work gets missed or overwritten, and the first session turns into a questionnaire.
