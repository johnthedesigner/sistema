## Why

Motion that explains a change (where a menu came from, that something was saved) helps; motion for its own sake makes a product feel slow. Ease-out starts fast and settles, which feels responsive. Interactions repeated hundreds of times a day, such as a command menu, feel slower with entrance motion.

## When you'd choose otherwise

A consumer or brand-led product where motion is part of the character.

## If you override it

Record the motion character in DECISIONS.md; reduced-motion handling still applies.
