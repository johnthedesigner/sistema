## Why

Agents report progress that turns out to be partial. A claim costs nothing to make. A passing build or a rendered page can be checked by anyone.

## What breaks without it

Every approval in Sistema rests on something you can see or check. Without evidence, "done" is only the agent's word, and problems surface later, when they cost more to fix.
