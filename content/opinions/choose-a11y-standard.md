## Why

Requirements differ by use. A public service and an internal tool have different obligations. Choosing a standard up front means every check measures against the right numbers. Going beyond the minimum is encouraged where it's cheap, and that advice depends on context.

## When you'd choose otherwise

Your organization already sets a standard. Record it as the project's choice.

## If you override it

There is always a standard. Overriding means choosing a different one, which is logged like any decision.
