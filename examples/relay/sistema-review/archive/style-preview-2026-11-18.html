Relay — style preview

Visual direction for review before any token values are generated. Light theme is primary; dark theme is shown in Color in Context.

Visual Direction

  • Aesthetic reference: Functionalist (Rams) as the primary framework: every mark carries information, nothing decorates. Swiss/International typographic hierarchy for the data: weight and case, not size, separate roles. A borrowed note from field-service signage and equipment labelling: one deep petrol "service" color, compact uppercase labels, status shown with a filled dot plus a word.
  • Color commitment: Level 1 — Restrained, with hue-tinted neutrals (neutral chroma about 0.01 at hue 215 to 225, matching the primary). Dispatchers stare at this for eight hours, and status color must stay the loudest thing on screen, so chrome stays quiet.
  • Typographic character: One family for UI and one matching mono for data: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono. Weights 400 to 700; body 14px, table data 13px, labels 12px semibold or 11px uppercase. Hierarchy comes from weight and case; the largest size in daily use is 20px.
  • Shape personality: Sharp-precise: 2px chips, 4px buttons and inputs, 6px panels and popovers; full radius only for status dots and avatars.
  • What this is not: not a medium-blue SaaS template with 8px-rounded cards and soft shadows; not a "dark sophisticated" console; not brutalist (no 2px black borders, no all-monospace).

Review Checklist

Before approving this visual direction, review these specific decisions:

  • [ ] Primary color: petrol, seed oklch(0.50 0.09 210), primary 600 #006D7A in light, primary 400 #1E9FB1 in dark. Chosen to sit outside the medium-blue SaaS default (220 to 240), to nod to water and ductwork, and to stay clearly apart from the status hues (green, amber, red, blue).
  • [ ] Info blue vs. primary petrol: hues 264 and 210. Check that "En route" chips and primary buttons never read as the same thing.
  • [ ] Surface commitment: Level 1 — Restrained, with a faint cool tint (chroma about 0.01). Light surface neutral 50 #F9FAFA is close to the "#FAFAFA" reflex; it is kept because the canvas (neutral 100) carries the tint and table surfaces need maximum contrast. Say if you want a warmer or more visibly tinted canvas.
  • [ ] Radius personality: 2 / 3 / 4 / 6px, sharp-precise. Does this feel precise rather than harsh?
  • [ ] Typeface: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next with its Mono. Chosen for character distinction (1, I, l, 0, O) in job numbers, phone numbers, and addresses read all day. Confirm the character suits Relay; alternatives are IBM Plex Sans or Public Sans.
  • [ ] Status chips use a dot, a word, and a tinted container, never color alone.
  • [ ] Selected table row: primary-container fill plus a 2px primary inset on the first cell (an inset edge on a selected row, not a decorative side stripe).
  • [ ] Contrast: all text pairings pass 4.5:1. The light focus ring (primary 500) is 4.19:1 on the surface, which is enough for a UI part (3:1).

Decisions applied from defaults (brief was underspecified):

  • [ ] No secondary brand hue (brief named no brand). shadcn/ui "secondary" maps to neutrals.
  • [ ] Status vocabulary and colors (on site = success, en route = info, late = warning, parts hold = error, unassigned = neutral) are examples; the real status list should come from how Relay's dispatchers work.
  • [ ] Feedback hues taken as the library's red, amber, green, and blue ramps unchanged.

These are the decisions to change before token generation begins.
