One trade-off to look at

Without outlines, panels are set off from the canvas by tone alone (about 1.15:1 in both themes), and in dark theme the status chip fills differ from the panel by hue more than by lightness. Neither edge is required by WCAG, because the chip's dot and word carry its meaning and a panel's edge isn't needed to use it, but they are quieter than before. If you'd like panels to read more clearly, the next step would be a slightly darker canvas tone, not the outline back.
