# Invisible details of interaction design (Rauno Freiberg)

Source: Rauno Freiberg, "Invisible Details of Interaction Design". https://rauno.me/craft/interaction-design

Sections cover metaphors, kinetic physics, gestures, spatial consistency, fluid morphing, frequency and novelty, fidgetability, scroll landmarks, implicit input, Fitts's law, and scrolling. The parts relevant to a design system's defaults:

- **Frequency and novelty:** whether to animate depends on how often an interaction happens. Command menus and context menus used hundreds of times a day feel slower, not better, with entrance motion; keyboard interactions especially can go without motion. macOS context menus appear instantly but fade out, with a brief accent blink on the chosen item as confirmation.
- **Spatial consistency:** things should move from and return to where they came from, so people keep a sense of place.
- **Closing idea:** being able to say why something feels right (a spring curve, a metaphor) is how taste grows; "it feels nice" isn't an explanation.

## Relevance to Sistema

Motion defaults by frequency (no entrance motion on frequent keyboard-driven surfaces), feeding the craft baseline's motion item and the motion guide.
