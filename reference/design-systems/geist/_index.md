## Overview

Geist is Vercel's design system for its own products (vercel.com, Next.js, v0). Its documentation is public; no open licence is stated for it, so it is tier 2. Its materials model (radius, fill, stroke, and shadow bundled by elevation role) and color steps with assigned jobs are useful references for contemporary, restrained product surfaces.

**When to reference:** surfaces and elevation as named roles; radius that grows with elevation; restrained border use.
