# Adobe Spectrum 2: attention hierarchy

Source: https://spectrum.adobe.com/foundations/attention-hierarchy

- Spectrum 2 treats design systems as communication systems that must be "respectful of user attention".
- **Attention hierarchy** is a core Spectrum 2 concept, modelled on typographic hierarchy (scale, size, and pairing tell the reader what matters most).
- It is framed as managing the **signal-to-noise ratio**. High-attention elements mark the primary focus and carry timely, high-signal meaning. Low-attention elements blend into the background and carry utilitarian, lower-priority meaning. Object styles and component options are chosen with this in mind.
- High-attention treatments are used **sparingly and selectively**: both high-contrast color and elevation add emphasis and are used "very thoughtfully".
- As a general rule, **only one element or group should have high attention on a page**; too many makes the page noisy and unfocused.
