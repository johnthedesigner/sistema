## Overview

Apple's Human Interface Guidelines cover design for Apple platforms. They are official public documentation with no open licence (tier 2). Platform-specific detail (Liquid Glass, size classes) is outside Sistema's web scope; the principles on layering, materials by meaning, label contrast levels, and grouping carry over.

**When to reference:** separating controls from content; text tone ladders; choosing surfaces by role.
